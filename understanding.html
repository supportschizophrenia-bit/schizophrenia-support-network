Understanding Schizophrenia

Exploring lived experience, phenomenology, and holistic perspectives

Understanding schizophrenia requires moving beyond medical labels to engage with the lived experience of those affected. Here, we gather resources from multiple perspectives to provide a fuller picture.

Our Approach to Resources

We focus on:

  • Phenomenology: What is it actually like to experience schizophrenia?
  • Holism: Understanding the whole person, not just symptoms
  • Dignity: Resources that respect the person, not just the diagnosis
  • Diversity: Multiple perspectives, experiences, and approaches to understanding
  • Accessibility: Clear, jargon-free language where possible
