Practitioner Profile

Dr Faye Doell

Clinical Psychologist

Country: Canada

Languages: English

About Me

Dr. Faye Doell, Ph.D. is a registered psychologist who received her doctoral degree in Clinical Psychology from York University in Toronto, Canada. Dr. Doell served as the Clinical Lead of the Cognitive Behaviour Therapy for Psychosis (CBTp) Service at the Centre for Addiction and Mental Health (CAMH), Canada’s largest mental health and addition-focused hospital, for over a decade, and is currently the Clinical and Training Director at Laksman Doell Psychology. At CAMH, she was responsible for developing group and individual programming in CBTp, providing leadership for capacity building initiatives, provision of consultation to physicians, psychologists and allied mental health staff, and the establishment of a comprehensive training program in CBTp and CBTp-informed care. Outside of her work at CAMH, Dr. Doell has consulted on implementation strategies for CBTp in other health care settings, has presented widely on CBTp, has been an invited contributing expert on government-led mental health initiatives related to CBTp. She is a Founding Member and current Co-Vice President of the North America CBT for Psychosis Network (NACBTpN).

My Approach

Dr. Doell’s early clinical experiences and training in the area of severe and complex mental health issues has led her to concentrate her focus on adults who are living with longstanding and complex difficulties. Her career has been dedicated to helping individuals who have historically not had access to evidence-based psychotherapy, and to helping individuals who are struggling to make sustained and meaningful changes in their lives.

Qualifications

Dr. Doell completed both her Master’s and Doctoral degrees in Clinical Psychology at York University in Toronto. She has demonstrated expertise in training, program development and implementation of cognitive behavioural therapy for psychosis (CBTp), and she served in a leadership role for the Cognitive Behaviour Therapy for Psychosis Service at the Centre for Addiction and Mental Health (CAMH), Canada’s largest mental health and addiction-focused hospital, for over a decade.
