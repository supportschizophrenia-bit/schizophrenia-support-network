# Schizophrenia Support Network

A comprehensive online community and resource platform dedicated to supporting people diagnosed with schizophrenia in India.

## Mission

Transforming how schizophrenia is understood, discussed, and treated in India by:
- Centering dignity and human worth
- Supporting healing and self-fulfillment, not just adjustment
- Prioritizing lived experiences and phenomenology
- Providing accessible resources and community support

## Site Structure

### Pages
- **Home (index.html)** - Overview of the organization and initiatives
- **About Us (about.html)** - Mission, values, and approach
- **Understanding Schizophrenia (understanding.html)** - Educational resources organized by perspective
- **Practitioner Directory (practitioners.html)** - Database of practitioners and registration form
- **Support Community (support-community.html)** - Links to support channels and community guidelines
- **Contact (contact.html)** - Contact information and crisis resources

## Support Channels

1. **Weekly Zoom Support Groups** - Moderated groups for people with schizophrenia and families
2. **Reddit Community** - Open discussions and peer support
3. **Instagram** - Daily resources, inspiration, and community highlights
4. **Facebook Support Group** - Community discussion and support

## Resource Categories

- Lived Experiences & Phenomenology
- Symptomological Perspectives
- Psychosocial Understanding
- Physiological & Somatic Perspectives
- Recovery & Healing
- Resources for Families

## Key Features

- **Practitioner Registration**: Google Form integration for adding practitioners to the directory
- **Community Links**: Easy access to all support channels
- **Educational Resources**: Organized by multiple perspectives (not just medical)
- **Family Support**: Specific resources for caregivers and loved ones
- **Crisis Resources**: Emergency contact information

## Content Strategy

- Focus on phenomenology and lived experience
- Avoid over-reliance on medicalization
- Center human dignity and potential
- Support self-fulfillment and meaning-making, not just symptom management
- Accessible language and inclusive approach

## Setup Instructions

1. **Update Practitioner Registration Form**: Replace the Google Form link in `practitioners.html` with your actual form URL
2. **Update Social Media Links**: Update Instagram, Facebook, and Reddit links throughout the site
3. **Contact Information**: Update the email address in `contact.html` and footer
4. **Crisis Resources**: Keep India-specific helpline numbers current
5. **Add Content**: Add articles, resources, and practitioner entries as they are created
