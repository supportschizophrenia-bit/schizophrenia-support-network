Practitioner Directory

Connect with compassionate, experienced practitioners across India

Find Support & Treatment

Our directory includes psychiatrists, therapists, counselors, and support workers who are experienced with schizophrenia and committed to compassionate, holistic care. Practitioners may specialize in:

  • Clinical support and medication management
  • Psychotherapy and counseling
  • Family therapy and support
  • Peer support and peer-led recovery
  • Holistic and complementary approaches
  • Occupational and vocational support

Add a Practitioner to Our Directory

Know a practitioner who should be listed? We welcome nominations and self-registrations from:

  • Psychiatrists and mental health doctors
  • Psychologists and therapists
  • Counselors and social workers
  • Peer support specialists and lived experience facilitators
  • Holistic and alternative practitioners with experience supporting people with schizophrenia
