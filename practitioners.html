Find practitioners & supporters

Living with schizophrenia, psychosis, voice hearing, unusual experiences, emotional distress, or recovery after a mental health crisis can be challenging and often confusing. Many people discover that there is no single approach that works for everyone. What is helpful during one stage of recovery may not be what is needed at another stage. Some people benefit from medication. Others find support through therapy, peer support, meaningful work, physical health practices, spiritual exploration, community connection, creative expression, or a combination of different approaches. This directory has been created to help you explore a wide range of support options and connect with practitioners who may be relevant to your current needs. Think of this platform as a guide or navigator. Rather than telling you what path to follow, we aim to help you discover the different forms of support that are available and make informed choices about your own recovery journey.

Do you offer holistic, phenomenological, or peer-based support? Join our directory to connect with those seeking compassionate care.
