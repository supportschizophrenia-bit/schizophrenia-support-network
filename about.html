About Schizophrenia Support Network

Our mission, values, and approach to holistic support

Our Mission

The Schizophrenia Support Network is dedicated to transforming how schizophrenia is understood, discussed, and treated in India. We believe that people diagnosed with schizophrenia deserve:

  • Dignity and Respect: Rejecting stigma and recognizing inherent human worth
  • Comprehensive Support: Access to treatment, healing resources, and community
  • Self-Fulfillment: Opportunities to pursue meaning, purpose, and self-actualization
  • Holistic Understanding: Resources that address symptomological, psychosocial, physiological, and somatic aspects
  • Lived Experience Focus: Learning from and centering the phenomenology of people with schizophrenia

Why We Exist

The dialogue around schizophrenia in India often centers on adjustment, management, and survival. While stability is important, it should not be the ceiling of aspiration. People with schizophrenia can—and should—be supported in:

  • Healing from trauma and distress
  • Discovering and pursuing their purpose
  • Achieving self-actualization and meaningful living
  • Connecting with community and belonging

What We Offer

📚 Educational Resources

Articles, readings, and materials that explore schizophrenia from multiple perspectives without over-reliance on medicalization.

👨‍⚕️ Practitioner Directory

A curated database of therapists, counselors, psychiatrists, and support workers across India who are experienced and compassionate.

🤝 Community Support

Multiple channels for connection: weekly Zoom groups, Reddit, Instagram, and Facebook communities.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Resources

Support and education specifically for families navigating schizophrenia, emphasizing understanding and compassionate care.

Our Values

💝 Human Dignity

Every person deserves respect, regardless of diagnosis.

🌟 Holism

We address the whole person: mind, body, psyche, and social context.

💪 Empowerment

We support agency, choice, and self-determination.

🌍 Community

We believe in the healing power of connection and belonging.

🔓 Accessibility

Resources and support should be available to all, regardless of background or location.
