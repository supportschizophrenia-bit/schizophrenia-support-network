Looking for a Place to Belong

Reflection from the First Schizophrenia Support Group Meeting

30 May 2026

Three strangers came together to speak openly about schizophrenia, family, loneliness, and the longing to be understood. As the conversation unfolded, one quiet truth emerged: perhaps healing begins when we discover we no longer have to carry our experiences alone.

About these reflections

These reflections are written after each weekly Schizophrenia Support Network support group meeting. They are not transcripts, but thoughtful reflections on the themes that emerged during our conversations. Personal details have been removed or changed to protect confidentiality while preserving the humanity of what was shared.
