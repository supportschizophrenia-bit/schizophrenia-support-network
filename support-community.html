Community guidelines

Our community is founded on deep respect, phenomenal understanding, and mutual support. We prioritize human dignity and creating safe havens free from pathologizing language.

  • Respect the validity of each person's subjective reality and narrative.
  • Offer empathy and solidarity rather than attempting to diagnose or prescribe.
  • Maintain absolute privacy and safeguard the stories shared within the space.
  • Commit to de-stigmatizing dialogue and avoiding harmful medicalized labels.

For family members

Family members and caregivers are welcome in all our communities. We also offer resources specifically designed to support those caring for someone with schizophrenia.
