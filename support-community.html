Weekly Schizophrenia Support Group

Every Saturday we gather online to create a space where people living with schizophrenia, psychosis, and related experiences can speak openly without judgement. Our conversations are grounded in respect, curiosity, and the belief that every person's experience deserves to be heard.

🌿 A Safe Space

Our meetings are confidential and non-judgemental. Participants are welcome to share whatever feels important—from voices, unusual beliefs, medication, relationships, trauma, loneliness, hope, or simply how their week has been.

These meetings are not lectures or therapy sessions. They are conversations between people who listen deeply, learn from one another, and understand that every experience deserves dignity and respect.

The group is facilitated by Peer guides

Community Values

Respect

Every person's experience deserves to be heard without judgement or ridicule.

Curiosity

We seek to understand before we attempt to explain, diagnose, or correct.

Confidentiality

Stories shared within the group remain within the group.

Hope

Recovery is not only about reducing symptoms but also about rebuilding meaning, connection, and dignity.
