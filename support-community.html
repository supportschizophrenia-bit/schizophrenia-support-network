Join our weekly Support Group

Every Saturday we gather online to create a space where people living with schizophrenia and related psychotic experiences can speak openly without judgement. Whether you're newly diagnosed or have lived with schizophrenia for many years, you'll be welcomed with respect, curiosity, and compassion. Connection is a profound source of healing. Find solidarity, share your unique narrative, and build relationships with peers who honor your truth.

Who Can Join?

This group is open to people living with schizophrenia/schizoaffective or related psychotic experiences. Whether you have recently been diagnosed or have lived with schizophrenia for many years, you are welcome.

Practical Information

  • Every Saturday
  • 6:30 – 8:00 PM IST
  • Online via Zoom
  • Free to attend
  • Facilitated by Pankaj Suneja
  • Confidential and respectful space
Register for the next meeting →

What to Expect

These meetings are not therapy or lectures, but they are therapeutic in nature. They are conversations where people come together to share experiences, listen with care, and explore recovery, relationships, identity, voices, grief, hope, and everyday life—without judgement or pressure to speak.

Peer Support

Crisis Buddy Program

No one should have to face a difficult moment alone.

The Crisis Buddy Program is a peer-support initiative of the Schizophrenia Support Network that connects people with trained peers who can offer companionship, listening, and practical support during difficult moments, emotional distress, or the early signs of a crisis.

A Crisis Buddy are peers who chooses to walk alongside another person with empathy, patience, respect, and hope.

Community Values

Respect

Every person's experience deserves to be heard without judgement or ridicule.

Curiosity

We seek to understand before we attempt to explain, diagnose, or correct.

Confidentiality

Stories shared within the group remain within the group.

Hope

Recovery is not only about reducing symptoms but also about rebuilding meaning, connection, and dignity.
