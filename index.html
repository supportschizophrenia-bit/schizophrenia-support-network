Our Core Beliefs

🙋 Dignity & Worth

People with schizophrenia are not "garbage." They have inherent dignity, value, and potential.

✨ Beyond Adjustment

Recovery is not just about living a "well-adjusted" life. It includes healing, self-fulfillment, and pursuing meaning.

🎯 Purpose & Meaning

People with schizophrenia can strive toward their purpose, self-actualization, and meaningful living.

📖 Lived Experience

We prioritize phenomenology and lived experiences over medicalization. Understanding the person comes first.
