Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

The Schizophrenia Support Network is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our site. This Privacy Policy explains our information practices.

Information We Collect

We may collect information in the following ways:

  • Contact Forms: When you contact us, we collect your name, email, and message
  • Practitioner Registration: Information provided in the practitioner registration form
  • Automatic Information: We may collect information about your device and browsing behavior

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and messages
  • To manage the practitioner directory
  • To improve our website and services
  • To send updates and information about our community (with your consent)

Data Protection

We take the security of your information seriously. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Third-Party Services

Our site may include links to third-party services (Google Forms, social media platforms, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. Please review their privacy policies.

Community Privacy

In our support communities, we emphasize confidentiality: "What is shared stays in the group." However, please be aware that no online space is completely private. Share only what you are comfortable with.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of communications at any time

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at: info@schizophreniasupportnetwork.org

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
