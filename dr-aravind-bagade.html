Practitioner Profile

Dr. Aravind Bagade

Ayurvedic physician and Functional Medicine practitioner,

Country: India

Languages: English

About Me

Dr. Aravind Bagade is one of the founders of Hashtag Ayur Health and Wellness. He is a Mysore based Functional Medicine and Ayurveda practitioner with over 10 years’ experience. He is the Director of Health and Nutrition at Doman International Institute in Philadelphia and the Chief of Health at Hashtag Ayur in Mysore. He also teaches Ayurveda at Mysore Krishnamachar Yoga Shaala from the past 4 years. Dr Aravind Bagade’s Consultations are based on an Integrated Functional Medicine and Ayurvedic approaches. He also teaches Ayurveda and Functional Nutrition at Hashtag Ayur.

My Approach

Dr. Bagade meets patients with warmth and affection, and looks at the whole person, rather than reductionist approach. He stresses that each person's journey is unique. He supports people living with schizophrenia through an integrative perspective. He stresses on the relationship between gut health and the brain, nutrition, inflammation and environmental toxins, Genetics and epigenetics. He looks beyond symptoms with hope and curiousity. You may learn more about his approach toward schizophrenia here in this conversation https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WvOSiVeNOig&t=4s

Qualifications

Ayurvedic Physician — Trained in traditional Indian medicine, focusing on holistic, preventive, and lifestyle-based healthcare.

Functional Medicine Practitioner — Uses integrative, root‑cause approaches to chronic conditions, gut health, inflammation, and metabolic issues
